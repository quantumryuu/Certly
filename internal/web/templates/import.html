Import

Bring in CAs and certificates from your existing scripts so you can track, export and revoke them here. Import roots first, then intermediates, then leaf certificates: a certificate's issuer must already be in Certly (found by signature).

Keys are encrypted with the master key as soon as they're stored. A CA needs its key to issue certificates and publish CRLs here. Legacy encrypted PEM keys (“Proc-Type: 4,ENCRYPTED”) aren't accepted. Convert them first: openssl pkcs8 -topk8 -v2 aes-256-cbc -in old.key -out new.key. Certificates signed with SHA-1 or MD5 are rejected.
